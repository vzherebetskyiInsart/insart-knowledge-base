# Overview of Ukraine's Top Insurance Companies

Ukraine’s insurance sector has experienced rapid growth and increased profitability following the shock of the full-scale war, with non-life premiums reaching UAH 65.6 billion. The market is highly concentrated, with the top insurers accounting for the vast majority of premiums and payouts.

## Industry Leaders and Market Overview

The Ukrainian insurance market is primarily divided into Life and Non-Life (Risk) segments, with motor insurance (CASCO, MTPL, and Green Card) serving as the primary driver of growth.

### Top Life Insurance Companies

- **MetLife Ukraine:** Leading the life segment, it became the most profitable life insurer in the country, earning approximately UAH 977 million in recent annual profits.
- **TAS Life:** Consistently in the top tier for premium collection and profitability.
- **PZU Ukraine Life:** A subsidiary of the Polish PZU Group, noted for high financial stability and reliable payouts.

### Top Non-Life (Risk) Insurance Companies

- **ARX (formerly AXA Ukraine):** One of the largest non-life insurers, consistently ranking at the top for payout stability, CASCO, and comprehensive coverage.
- **TAS Insurance Group:** One of the most profitable groups in Ukraine, holding a major share of the auto and voluntary health insurance markets.
- **PZU Ukraine:** One of the largest insurers in the market, heavily backed by foreign capital and playing a significant role in Ukraine's economic rebuilding efforts.
- **INGO:** A major player handling significant volumes of corporate insurance, medical, and Green Card policies.
- **USG (Ukrainian Insurance Group):** Holds a substantial market share and is recognized for high MTPL (Auto) payouts and liquidity.

## Market Trends and Financial Stability

- **Profitability and Assets:** Industry assets have increased significantly, with high-quality liquid assets strengthening the sector's ability to meet financial obligations.
- **Foreign Investment:** A large percentage of the sector's profits are generated by companies with foreign beneficiaries, signaling strong international confidence in Ukraine's financial system.
- **War Risk Insurance:** Leading companies have adapted to the climate by offering innovative solutions for both business and individual war-risk coverage, supported by international backing and export credit agencies.

## Useful Resources and Official Statistics

To verify current financial ratings and performance metrics, or to find a policy, consider visiting these official platforms:

- **[National Bank of Ukraine (NBU)](https://bank.gov.ua):** Official regulatory body that publishes insurance market statistics, licensing details, and insurer financial statements.
- **[National Association of Insurers of Ukraine (NASU)](https://nasu.com.ua/en/):** Represents major drivers of the Ukrainian insurance market and provides up-to-date industry analytics.
- **[Forinsurer](https://forinsurer.com/):** A leading industry portal offering ratings, news, and financial overviews of Ukrainian insurance companies.
